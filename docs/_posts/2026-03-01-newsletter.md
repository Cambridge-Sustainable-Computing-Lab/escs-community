### February & March Recap of the ESCS Community Forum

**News & Resources 📰**

- **US state has introduced a bill halting all data centre development to prioritise policy regulations.** The state of Georgia has introduced a bill that will pause all plans to set up new data centres until March 2027, in order “to allow state, county and municipal-level officials time to set necessary policies for regulating data centers … which permanently alter the landscape of our state”. [Read the article here](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jan/26/georgia-datacenters-ai-ban). <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/us-state-has-introduced-a-bill-halting-all-data-centre-development-to-prioritise-policy-regulations/246)

- **New ‘Sustainable computing in science’ training course.** Published by [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/) and the [Cambridge Sustainable Computing Lab](https://cam-sustainablecomputing.org/) at the University of Cambridge with funding support from the [Wellcome Trust](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wellcome-trust), [the course](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/online/courses/sustainable-computing-in-science/) covers the environmental impacts of computational research, quantifying these impacts, and questioning how compute users can reduce the footprint of their work. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/sustainable-computing-in-science-training-course-by-embl-ebi-and-the-cambridge-sustainable-computing-lab/247)

**Upcoming Events 🎟️**

- **NetDRIVE meeting on ‘Creating a New Reality: A Sustainable Digital Transformation’.** From 22-23 April 2026, NetDRIVE will be holding its next meeting in the University of Oxford on the emerging UK power supply and broader questions of community leadership, scientific credibility, and digital transformation in support of net zero. Registration is free, [learn more and sign up here.](https://uknetdrive.org/events/creating-a-new-reality-a-sustainable-digital-transformation)

- **From climate change to energy securitisation: Advancing the green transition amid shifting narratives of power and crisis.** CRASSH, the Centre for Research in the Arts, Social Sciences and Humanities is organising this event on the 29th of April 2026 as part of its 25th anniversary in Cambridge, UK. [Learn more and register here.](https://www.cfg.cam.ac.uk/events/from-climate-change-to-energy-securitisation-advancing-the-green-transition-amid-shifting-narratives-of-power-and-crisis)

- **SC4RC, the first interdisciplinary conference on environmentally sustainable research computing from 4-8 May at CERN in Geneva, Switzerland is open for registration and abstract submissions.** Learn more on the [website](https://www.sc4rc.org/) and register through [this link](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1526482). <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/excited-to-announce-sc4rc-2026-the-first-interdisciplinary-conference-on-environmentally-sustainable-research-computing-happening-in-geneva-switzerland-on-4-8-may-2026/219)

- **CECAM-CSCS is running EcoCompute: Building Sustainable Scientific Computing Practices through Academia-Industry Collaboration.** This workshop on environmental sustainability will run from 5-7 May in CSCS, Lugano, Switzerland. [Register here](https://www.cecam.org/workshop-details/ecocompute-building-sustainable-scientific-computing-practices-through-academia-industry-collaboration-1475). <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/ecocompute-cecam-cscs-workshop-5-7-may-2026-lugano-switzerland-and-online/242)

- **Making AI (truly) sustainable - from environmental costs to social impacts.** As part of the Turing Lectures, the Alan Turing Institute is organising this event in the British Library in London on the 27th of May 2026 led by Dr Sasha Luccioni, Climate Lead at global responsible AI start-up Hugging Face. [Learn more and register in-person or online (free) here.](https://www.turing.ac.uk/events/turing-lectures-making-ai-truly-sustainable-environmental-costs-social-impacts)

- **Applications are open for the E4C - Green IT Summer School 2026 in the Paris region from 22-26 June.** Designed for students and industry professionals interested in sustainable cloud computing, responsible data centres, eco-designed software, and green artificial intelligence, applications are now open until 30 April. Financial support is also available. [Learn more here.](https://e4c.ip-paris.fr/en/formations/autres-actions-pedagogiques-et-soft-skills/summer-schools/e4c-green-it-summer-school-2026)

- **EESP Workshop 2026 on Energy Efficiency with Sustainable Performance: Techniques, Tools, and Best Practices.** Call for papers on a range of topics of interest relating to sustainable computing are open for this workshop on the 26th of June 2026 in Hamburg, Germany. [Learn more here.](https://ayeshaafzal91.github.io/eesp)

🌱 ***What is this newsletter?*** *This aims to be a round up of interesting news and developments in the sustainable computing space. Whenever we come across an interesting news item or publication on the topic, we will post it on the forum so that everyone can jump in and discuss it. Of course anyone’s welcome to post as well! At the end of each month, we will send out this monthly digest of the articles shared and of the discussions that happened on the forum.*
