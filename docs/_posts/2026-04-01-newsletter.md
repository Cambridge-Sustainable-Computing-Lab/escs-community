### April & May Recap of the ESCS Community Forum

**News & Resources 📰**

- **Crewdle: a green and private alternative to Zoom.** Kirsty Pringle has shared a videoconferencing tool worth checking out for those looking to move away from Zoom for a more environmentally sustainable option. [Learn more here.](https://connect.crewdle.com/) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/crewdle-alternative-to-zoom-greener/256)

- **Call for survey responses: how scientists perceive “new” research practices in open science, slow science, green science, and its barriers.** Dom Makowski from the University of Sussex and collaborators from Paris are working to build an accurate picture of how contributing factors like contract precarity, institutional incentives, social inequalities, training opportunities, etc. impact science practices. Open to individuals across any discipline in any career stage and only takes 10mins max, [take the survey here.](https://limesurvey-lettres.sorbonne-universite.fr/index.php/518485?lang=en) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/study-on-barriers-to-culture-change-in-science-green-science-open-science-slow-science/258)

- **Session recordings from SC4RC, a conference dedicated to sustainable computing, are now available.** Earlier this month, the Sustainability Conference for Responsible Research Computing took place at CERN in Geneva, Switzerland. Over 150+ researchers and professionals working in the sustainable computing space came together in person and online. Session recordings are now available online. To see what the speakers and participants have to say about the event and their contributions, check the LinkedIn posts [here.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sc4rc) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/session-recordings-from-sc4rc-a-conference-dedicated-to-sustainable-computing-are-now-available/262)

- **Free, self-paced courses on Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) by the Green Software Foundation.** GSF has recently released online and free courses to support software developers on their sustainability journey. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/free-self-paced-courses-on-software-carbon-intensity-sci-by-the-green-software-foundation/261)

- **Green RSE Prize.** To encourage and promote the role of research software engineers who work on improving the environmental sustainability of research, the Software Sustainability Institute sponsors the Green RSE Award — £100 in prize money and a nice certificate. Want to nominate someone? [Follow this link!](https://www.software.ac.uk/news/green-rse-award-returns-2026) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/green-rse-prize-yes-a-prize-how-exciting/260)

- **More than 100 UK data centres plan to burn gas to generate electricity.** The Guardian has been conducting interesting (and depression) work on the energy usage of data centres in the UK and their environmental impacts, this is the latest in the series. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/more-than-100-uk-datacentres-plan-to-burn-gas-to-generate-electricity-the-guardian/264)

**Upcoming Events 🎟️**

- **Green Web Foundation’s State of the fossil-free Internet: online Public Briefing happening on 27 May.** The Green Web Foundation will be talking about their [State of the Fossil-Free Internet report](https://pretix.eu/redirect/?url=https%3A//fossilfree.greenweb.org/2026%3AsEV6uVXp8xyQKzQY6PLvhxjQDUDrztXvMlTIQG0N72Q) over Zoom next week. [Register here.](https://pretix.eu/greenweb/fossil-free) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://pretix.eu/greenweb/fossil-free)

- **Call for LOCO Workshop (Lancaster, UK and online) papers and lightning talks, deadline on 29 May.** The 2nd International Workshop on Low Carbon Computing (LOCO 2026) is taking place on 10 - 11 September 2026 in Lancaster (UK) and online. The main focus is on the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions from computing, including but not limited to low energy and energy efficiency; recognising the need for long lived low footprint systems, and computing’s wider role in amplifying climate impacts in other societal systems. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/call-for-loco-workshop-lancaster-uk-and-online-papers-and-lightning-talks-deadline-29-may/266)

- **IEA Webinar on 1 June: Key Questions on Energy and AI.** The IEA is hosting a webinar on its new report examining how the energy and AI nexus has evolved amidst surging investments into data centres and advances in model capabilities. Sign up to the webinar [here.](https://www.iea.org/events/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai-webinar) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/iea-key-questions-on-energy-and-ai-webinar/267)

- **Call for Papers by 8 June - UK AI Research Symposium in Edinburgh in Nov 2026.** The UK AI Research Symposium (UKAIRS) will have a track on “Sustainability and AI”. This symposium is one of the premier national gathering for the UK artificial intelligence research community, and is organized in collaboration with UKRI, RAI UK, the national AI Hubs, and CDT centres. Check the link below for topics and eligibility. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/call-for-papers-uk-ai-research-symposium/259)

- **In-person and online ICT4S Workshops 2026 Bern, Switzerland on 12 June.** This year’s ICT for Sustainability Conference (ICT4S) hosts several independent workshops that offer in-depth exploration of specific topics: Multi-Disciplinary Strategies for Greening Software Engineering, Assessment of Indirect Effects of Digitalisation (WAID): Can ICT Drive Sustainability, and the True Cost of ICT: Crisis of Abundance. [Check the programme](https://conf.researchr.org/track/ict4s-2026/ict4s-2026-workshops) and [register here.](https://conf.researchr.org/attending/ict4s-2026/registration) <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/ict4s-workshops-2026-participate-in-person-bern-switzerland-or-online/263)

- **Expressions of interest for a summer hackathon for the Climate Aware Task Scheduler (CATS).** The team behind CATS is organising a 4-day ~20 person hackathon in Oxford/online this summer (date TBC) and they are looking for people interested in attending (travel and accommodation can be covered). If you want to get involved, [fill up this form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/e/X7SCnHXyLm) or ask your questions through the link below. <br>
  👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/summer-hackathon-for-the-climate-aware-task-scheduler-expressions-of-interest/268)

### Know someone curious about sustainable computing? Help us grow the ESCS Community! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

If you haven’t already, join the [ESCS Forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/categories) — an online forum to connect with others on green computing topics whether you’re new to the space or have been in this domain for years. 🌱 ***What is this newsletter?*** *This aims to be a round up of interesting news and developments in the sustainable computing space. Whenever we come across an interesting news item or publication on the topic, we will post it on the forum so that everyone can jump in and discuss it. Of course anyone’s welcome to post as well! At the end of each month, we will send out this monthly digest of the articles shared and of the discussions that happened on the forum.*
