### July Recap of the ESCS Community Forum

**News & Resources 📰**

- **The Green DiSC Silver criteria for Research Groups is out now!** The certification scheme has now launched the Silver criteria for Research Groups looking to take the next step in their digital sustainability journey. All groups that have completed Bronze are eligible to apply for Silver. Email the Green DiSC team at info@greendisc.org to know more and even get involved in the development of Gold! <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/green-disc-silver-criteria-for-research-groups-is-now-out-gold-coming-soon/281)

- **New York becomes the first state to impose one-year pause on new AI data centres - The Guardian.** New York has imposed a moratorium on hyperscale date centres (>50 megawatts). This would allow state regulators to create a framework that is focused on data centres’ environmental impacts, energy demand, water use, etc. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/new-york-becomes-first-state-imposes-one-year-pause-on-new-ai-data-centres-the-guardian/285)

- **Upcoming training material on Sustainable Computing.** Noam Weingarten has shared the [GreenDIGIT Comprehensive Training Modules Developments to Support Sustainable Research Infrastructures](https://greendigit-project.eu/greendigit-comprehensive-training-modules-developments-to-support-sustainable-research-infrastructures/), which is designed to help Research Infrastructures (RIs) assess, understand, and improve the environmental sustainability of their digital services and operations. The project will be 20 training modules to be published on SoBigData Academy as a single Green DIGIT course and will be available by the end of August 2026. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/upcoming-training-material-on-sustainable-computing/275)

- **GreenDIGIT resource.** Noam Weingarten has also shared resources from [GreenDIGIT](https://greendigit-project.eu/), consisting of [4 Digital Infrastructures](https://greendigit-project.eu/our-infrastructures/) designed to address the needs of ESFRI Research Infrastructures and other digital service providers for science in lowering energy consumption and environmental impact: [EGI](https://www.egi.eu/egi-infrastructure/), [SoBigData](https://www.sobigdata.eu/), [SLICES](https://www.slices-ri.eu/), and [EBRAINS](https://ebrains.eu/). <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/greendigit-resource/283)

**Upcoming Events 🎟️**

- **Call for papers: Sustainable Digital Research Infrastructures for the Edge-to-Cloud Continuum.** This special issue of Elsevier Computer Communications will focus on sustainable research infrastructures for the cloud continuum, spanning the physical layer (sites, energy and cooling), the network layer (transport, edge access, monitoring), virtualization and orchestration (VMs, containers, serverless, NFV), data management and scientific workflows, as well as governance, policy, and green-by-design methodologies. Submissions are open until 30 August 2026. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/cfp-elsevier-computer-communications-special-issue-on-sustainable-edge-to-cloud-infrastructures-deadline-aug-30/286)

**Q&A Discussions 💬**

- **External HPC Computing Infrastructure Inventory without PUE: Using the Green500 List.** For those working on Green DiSC, Marco Huebner has shared an approach for researchers that use external supercomputing facilities that do not publish system-specific PUE value. [The Green500 list](https://top500.org/lists/green500/list/2026/06/) reports computing energy efficiency in GFLOPS/Watt. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/external-hpc-computing-infrastructure-inventory-without-pue-using-the-green500-list/284)

### Spread the word on the ESCS Community! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

If you’ve come across us recently, welcome! We hope this newsletter is connecting you to the right resources. And if you’ve been subscribed for a while now, we appreciate your support.

If you haven’t yet, we encourage you to get involved in the [ESCS Forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/categories) where you can connect with others on green computing topics regardless of whether you’re new to the space or have been in this domain for years. Our Community Manager, Anica, highlights new posts on the forum in this monthly newsletter. Your post will get featured in the next one!

And if you know someone looking for other folks interested in sustainable computing, invite them to the forum and forward this newsletter!

🌱 ***What is this newsletter?*** *This aims to be a round up of interesting news and developments in the sustainable computing space. Whenever we come across an interesting news item or publication on the topic, we will post it on the forum so that everyone can jump in and discuss it. Of course anyone’s welcome to post as well! At the end of each month, we will send out this monthly digest of the articles shared and of the discussions that happened on the forum.*
