### June Recap of the ESCS Community Forum

**News & Resources 📰**

- **Call for respondents: Research survey - carbon emission estimation tools for HPC.** Have you ever wondered what the carbon emissions of your HPC jobs are? Liz Ing-Simmons and her team are conducting a 5-10min survey to explore the usage of these tools, and the factors that motivate or impede their adoption. This survey is part of the “REACT: Recommendations for Enhancing Adoption of Carbon-footprinting Tools” research study, a [NetDRIVE](https://uknetdrive.org/) community project. [Fill out the survey here.](https://qualtrics.kcl.ac.uk/jfe/form/SV_6Jw4ljphZbF28aW) <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/research-survey-carbon-emission-estimation-tools-for-hpc/273)

- **A preprint about “The data heat island effect”.** Loïc Lannelongue shared a [preprint by Marinori et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20897) describing data centres’ impact on warming the air in their environments, one highlight being that temperature increases of hyperscalers are not just on the immediate neighbourhood of the data centres but can actually be felt several kilometres away. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/a-preprint-about-the-data-heat-island-effect/274)

**Upcoming Events 🎟️**

- **DRI-EMIT workshop, 7th July 2026, attend online or in-person.** The team behind the DRI-EMIT (Emissions Measurement and Interpretation Training) project - a NetDRIVE funded community project - will be running a short workshop on the measurement and effective use of carbon emissions data in scientific computing. This free hybrid event will take place on Tuesday, 7 July from 9:30am -12:30pm, at Daresbury Laboratory, Rutherford Appleton Laboratory, and online. [Register here.](https://www.notion.so/Draft-June-2026-Newsletter-38032fe3003a80f584a7f70169cf5052?pvs=21) <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/dri-emit-workshop-7th-july-2026-attend-online-or-in-person/269)

- **Climate Change AI Virtual Summer School 2026 from July 19 to August 22, 2026.** CCAI is hosting a 5-week, fully online programme about different applications of AI for climate action via lectures and hands-on tutorial. Designed for a wide range of sectors and career stages, including researchers, practitioners, decision-makers, and students across academia, industry, entrepreneurship, civil society, and the public sector. 🌱 ***What is this newsletter?*** *This aims to be a round up of interesting news and developments in the sustainable computing space. Whenever we come across an interesting news item or publication on the topic, we will post it on the forum so that everyone can jump in and discuss it. Of course anyone’s welcome to post as well! At the end of each month, we will send out this monthly digest of the articles shared and of the discussions that happened on the forum.*
