### August & September Recap of the ESCS Community Forum

**News & Resources 📰**

- **‘Proposed London data centre ‘incompatible’ with net zero with 1m-tonne carbon footprint’.** [The Guardian reports](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/aug/21/proposed-london-datacentre-will-have-annual-carbon-footprint-of-27000-flights-to-new-york) the planned North Ockendon hyperscale data centre would emit 72M tonnes of CO2 over its 60-year lifespan, with 1M tonnes of emissions every year during full operations. If approved, it would be one of the biggest in Europe, taking up 218 hectares of land. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/proposed-london-data-centre-incompatible-with-net-zero-with-1m-tonne-carbon-footprint-the-guardian/294)

- **The environmental and public health impacts on residents around data centres.** “How are you supposed to sleep when your whole flat is vibrating and your bed is vibrating?” [The Nerve reports](https://www.thenerve.news/p/slough-data-centres-investigation-noise-illness-protest-microsoft) on the residents living in Slough, England — the second largest data centre hub in the world — and how their quality of living has declined rapidly in just over a year. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/the-nerve-reports-on-environmental-and-public-health-impacts-on-residents-around-the-slough-data-centre-hub/295)

- **Spotlighting the environmental impact of GPUs.** [Justine Calma’s article on The Verge, titled ‘Who’s afraid of the big, bad GPU?’](https://www.theverge.com/cs/features/937356/ai-data-center-gpu-environmental-impact), sheds light on a key component often overlooked in the AI data centre discourse — graphics processing units. The article focuses on how much facilities are putting a strain on local resources, demanding just as much water as humans consumed bottled water in 2025, to finding ways to process the e-waste produced. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/the-verge-spotlights-the-environmental-impact-of-gpus-amidst-data-centre-discourse/296)

- **‘What does your AI agent cost — in dollars & in carbon?’.** The Green Software Foundation released an [educational tool](https://green-software-foundation.github.io/reference-implementations/tools/gsf/agentic-ai-impact-explorer) to help users understand the energy, carbon, and water use behind an agentic AI model to raise awareness and promote the reduction of its environmental impacts. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/what-does-your-ai-agent-cost-in-dollars-in-carbon-educational-tool-by-the-green-software-foundation/297)

- **Teaching the rebound effect using cards.** Kirsty Pringle has shared a teaching resource called The Rebound Archetypes Cards developed by Laetitia Bornes and Marcia Tavares Smith to help designers and decision-makers consider rebound effects for design or decision processes. [They are free to download here](https://wp.lancs.ac.uk/paris-de/rebound-archetype-cards) but make sure to inform the creators when you download and use the cards! <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/teaching-the-rebound-effect/291)

**Upcoming Events 🎟️**

- **NetDRIVE Autumn Gathering in Bristol (UK) from 4-6 November 2026.** The NetDRIVE Autumn meeting will be hosted at the Digital Cultures Research Centre in UWE Bristol at Watershed. It will focus on galvanising collective action towards more sustainable digital research infrastructure (DRI). [Learn more here.](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/netdrive-autumn-meeting-in-bristol-uk/289#:~:text=See%20the%20full%20details%20of%20the%20call%20and%20instructions%20for%20applying%20here) Abstracts submissions have closed but you can still confirm participation by 6 Oct. Registration is free. <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/netdrive-autumn-meeting-in-bristol-uk/289)

- **Defining “Green” Leadership in Digital Research - Focus Group on 17 November 2026 in Edinburgh.** Kirsty Pringle and Liz Ing-Simmons have shared an upcoming focus group workshop bringing together researchers, digital Research Technical Professional leaders, educators, and sustainability experts to explore how digital Research Technical Professionals (dRTPs) can be supported to influence and accelerate environmental sustainability across digital research. Supporting by the DisCouRSE network, it will be held in-person at the Bayes Centre of the University of Edinburgh from 10am-4pm. Slots are limited. [Learn more](https://discourse-network.github.io/events/202611_greening_digital_careers) and [register here](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=_oivH5ipW0yTySEKEdmlwpofFxJMT1lCmEeJoickqdhUMFlFNktPUk00WVVHQkVXT1o1Tlk4SVBVWiQlQCN0PWcu). <br>
    👉 [Join the discussion on the forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/t/defining-green-leadership-in-digital-research-focus-group-in-edinburgh/290)

### We're keen to grow ESCS Community! 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

The [ESCS Forum](https://forum.escs-community.org/categories) is where we want to bring together folks across fields and disciplines interested in green computing, whether you’re new to the space or have been in this domain for years. Our Community Manager, Anica, highlights new posts on the forum in this monthly newsletter. So make a post and you will most likely get featured in the next one!

If you’re connecting with new people looking for something like this, invite them to the forum and forward this newsletter!

---

🌱 ***What is this newsletter?*** *This aims to be a round up of interesting news and developments in the sustainable computing space. Whenever we come across an interesting news item or publication on the topic, we will post it on the forum so that everyone can jump in and discuss it. Of course anyone’s welcome to post as well! At the end of each month, we will send out this monthly digest of the articles shared and of the discussions that happened on the forum.*
